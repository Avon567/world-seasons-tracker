About this tracker
Season boundaries are based on astronomical equinoxes and solstices, not meteorological seasons. Season names depend on hemisphere. A country's capital is used as the reference location, which is only an approximation for countries spanning the Equator or multiple time zones.

Astronomical data: US Naval Observatory API documentation . Country information: REST Countries . Time zone lookup: Open-Meteo .
